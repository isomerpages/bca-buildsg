<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><strong>Submit Your Registration</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Check your <a href="/ibuildsg-club/pinnacle-competition-2026/eligibility/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">eligibility</a>,
then complete the <a href="https://go.gov.sg/pinnaclecompetition2026" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">online registration form</a> by
27 August 2026, 11.59PM (SGT)</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Select Your Building</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Choose <a href="https://sleb.sg/Building/GreenMarkBuildingsDirectory" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">1 Green Mark building</a> in
Singapore that interests or inspires your team the most</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Choose <strong><u>1 question</u></strong> from your track to answer comprehensively</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Spotlight Building Features</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><strong>Pre-IHL Track</strong>: You must cover <strong>2 different features or methods</strong> for
your chosen question</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>IHL Track</strong>: You must cover <strong>3 different features or methods</strong> for
your chosen question</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Create Your Video</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Produce a video up to 3 minutes long</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Tell a story that connects the building's features to Singapore's development</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Use creative visuals and engaging storytelling</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Write Your Caption</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Craft a caption (maximum 350 words)</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Reinforce your video's key messages about your selected building and its
sustainability features</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Submit Your Entry</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>The following must be submitted before 9 September 2026, 11.59PM (SGT):</p>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Share your video and caption on Instagram</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Add <a href="https://www.instagram.com/ibuildsgclub/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">@ibuildsgclub</a> as
a collaborator for the post before publishing</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Ensure your Instagram account is set to ‘public’ for verification and/or
content sharing until 30 September 2026 11.59PM (SGT) or the official announcement
of winners, whichever is earlier.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>For team entries, all members must post the video and caption on their
respective Instagram account.</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p>Submit your entry through the online submission form, which will be made
available during the submission period.</p>
</li>
</ol>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>
<p>Participation in this Competition is free.</p>
