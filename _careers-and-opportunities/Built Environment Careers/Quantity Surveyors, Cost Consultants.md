<h4><strong>Quantity Surveyor</strong></h4>
<p>Shape the value behind every project.</p>
<p>Great buildings need great ideas. They also need smart decisions about
cost, value and resources.</p>
<p>Quantity Surveyors help project teams get the most from every dollar,
balancing ambitious designs with what is practical and achievable.</p>

<h4><strong>What Does a Quantity Surveyor Do?</strong></h4>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Estimate and manage the cost of construction projects</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Use digital tools and data to track costs and resources</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Advise clients on contracts and procurement</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Work with architects, engineers and project teams to balance design, cost
and value</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Manage project finances from concept to completion</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>Think numbers meet strategy – with real projects at the end of it.</p>
<p><strong>&nbsp;</strong>
</p>
<h4><strong>Why This Role Matters</strong></h4>
<p>Singapore continues to invest in new infrastructure, buildings and the
transformation of existing spaces.</p>
<p>Quantity Surveyors help make these projects possible by balancing cost,
value and resources to deliver the best outcomes.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<h4><strong>Did You Know?</strong></h4>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Quantity Surveying goes beyond calculating costs – it involved commercial
strategy, contracts and decision making.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Digital tools and data are changing how project costs are estimated, monitored
and managed.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Your decisions can influence a project from its earliest idea right through
to completion.</p>
</li>
